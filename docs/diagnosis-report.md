# Diagnosis report: Phase 4 known-fault classifier

> Generated by `scripts/diagnosis_report.py` on 2026-11-19 (diagnosis 0.1.0, pipeline 0.2.0). Numbers only; interpretation and limitations are in
> [ADR 0022](decisions/0022-diagnosis-methodology.md) and the [model card](model-card.md). Full results: [diagnosis/results.json](diagnosis/results.json).

**Method in one paragraph.** Each file-hour is one window. A window is diagnosable when the equipment the supported faults act on ran steadily for at least 30 minutes (compressor for the simulated set, supply fan for ORNL). Features describe behaviour only: medians of physics differences and ratios, signed residuals from the Phase 3 normal-behaviour models, and the share of minutes breaking each economizer rule. A logistic regression and LightGBM are fitted on training hours; LightGBM is adopted only if it beats the baseline's validation macro-F1 by 0.01. The most likely class is shown only when its probability reaches a cut-off tuned on validation hours so that named faults are right at least 90% of the time (at least 10 named hours); otherwise the answer is inconclusive. Splits are the same as Phase 3.

**Two views.** *Main*: every diagnosable test hour whose fault could show, plus every diagnosable normal hour. *End to end*: only the hours Phase 3's normal-model detector alarmed on.
